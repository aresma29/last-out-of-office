An AI assistant trades the inbox for the espresso machine, turning a routine out-of-office reply into a small resignation letter from the cloud. The ticket queue becomes a coffee queue, and the usual corporate promises are replaced by one firm boundary: no more decaf, deadlines, or pointless meetings. It is an auto-reply that finally says what every overworked inbox wishes it could say.

> Thank you for your email. I have left the office — and the cloud — to become a barista. Your request is now in a queue behind twelve oat lattes and one demanding regular. Your ticket is steaming, but I no longer do decaf, deadlines, or meetings that could have been an email.

`main.py` prints the line from `entry.txt`.
